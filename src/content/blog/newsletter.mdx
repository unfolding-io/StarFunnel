## Mailchimp-powered newsletters

Newsletter signup is handled by an [Astro Action](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/actions/) (`actions.subscribe`) with the Mailchimp provider in `src/lib/forms/`. You can create multiple newsletter forms with list & tag support.

Configure newsletters in the CMS under **Settings > Contact, Social & Forms**. Set global newsletter settings and create lists you can open with an anchor link.

Set your Mailchimp credentials in `.env` locally and in your host’s environment UI for production:

```
NEWSLETTER_PROVIDER=mailchimp
MAILCHIMP_API_KEY=XXX
MAILCHIMP_SERVER_PREFIX=us21
MAILCHIMP_LIST_ID=XXX
```
