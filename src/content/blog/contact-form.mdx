## Getting Your Contact Form Up & Running

The contact form is handled by an [Astro Action](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/actions/) (`actions.contact`) with providers in `src/lib/forms/`. Multiple topics are supported — each topic can have its own destination email or Slack channel. Open the dialog with a link to `href="#contact"`.

Configure the form in the CMS under **Settings > Contact, Social & Forms** (provider: `mailgun`, `postmark`, or `slack`). Set secrets in `.env` locally and in your host’s environment UI for production. `CONTACT_FORM_ENDPOINT` overrides the CMS provider when set. For Mailgun/Postmark, `FROM_EMAIL_ADDRESS` must be an authorized sender on your domain.

### Mailgun

```
CONTACT_FORM_ENDPOINT=mailgun
FROM_EMAIL_ADDRESS=hello@your-domain.com
TO_EMAIL_ADDRESS=hello@your-domain.com
MAILGUN_API_KEY=XXXXXXXXX
MAILGUN_DOMAIN=your-domain.com
MAILGUN_API_URL=https://api.eu.mailgun.net
```

`MAILGUN_API_URL` is `https://api.eu.mailgun.net` (EU) or `https://api.mailgun.net` (US) — do not include `/v3` (the action adds it).

### Postmark

Add your server token to the `.env`.

```
CONTACT_FORM_ENDPOINT=postmark
FROM_EMAIL_ADDRESS=hello@your-domain.com
TO_EMAIL_ADDRESS=hello@your-domain.com
POSTMARK_SERVER_TOKEN=XXXXXXXXX
```

### Slack

Create a Slack app, then add the token + channel id to your `.env`:

```
CONTACT_FORM_ENDPOINT=slack
SLACK_CHANNEL_ID=XXXXXXXXX
SLACK_TOKEN=XXXXXXXXX
```

**Create your Slack app:**

1. Go to [https://api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps)
2. Click “Create an App”
3. Select “from scratch”
4. Set name and workspace
5. Go to OAuth & Permissions
6. Add Scopes (`chat:write`, `chat:write:customize`, `chat:write:public`)
7. In the left settings menu select “Install app” and connect it to your workspace
8. Copy the **Bot User OAuth Token** — this is your `SLACK_TOKEN`
